Activity 6.5: Taking part in conversations at a railway station by talking/signing

(a) Act out the following conversation between Iku and Atu, who met at a railway station, by talking/signing.

Iku: Hi, I’m Iku.

Atu: Hi, I’m Atu.

Iku: Where are you going?

Atu: I’m going to Kigoma with my parents.

Iku: I am going to Dodoma.

I alight at the Dodoma Railway station.

Do you alight at the Kigoma station?

Atu: Oh, no.

We get out at Nguruka station.

Iku: What time do you arrive at Nguruka station?
