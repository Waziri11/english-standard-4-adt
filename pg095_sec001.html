Activity 11.2: Using punctuation marks appropriately
(a) Add a full stop or a question mark at the end of each sentence.
1. What is your name
2. My mother is a teacher
3. Do you like playing football
4. We went to the market yesterday
5. Can you help me with my homework
6. The sun rises in the east
(b) Add commas where necessary in the following sentences.
1. I have a pencil an eraser and a ruler in my bag.
2. We saw lions elephants and zebras at the zoo.
3. My favourite fruits are mangoes oranges and bananas.
4. During the holidays I visited my grandparents my cousins and my aunt.
5. On Monday Tuesday and Wednesday I go to school.
(c) Read the following sentences carefully.
Add the correct punctuation marks (full stop, comma, or question mark) where needed.
1. What is your favourite food
2. Juma likes to play football swim in the river and climb trees
