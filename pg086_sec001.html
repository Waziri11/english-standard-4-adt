My Saturday routine

My name is Malea. Over the weekend, I have time to stay with my siblings and parents at home. I wake up at 6.30 a.m. Our mother assigns to us different household activities. From 9:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m., I wash my school uniform. During the rainy season, it takes time for my clothes to get dry. My mother advises me to wash them earlier than 9:00 a.m. After washing my clothes, I help my mother to wash the dishes we use during breakfast in the morning. At around 11:30 a.m., my mother starts preparing some food for the family. I stay with my mother in the kitchen. I wish to help her cook ugali but she says I cannot manage to cook food for all people in the family. Our family has a lot of visitors on Saturdays. I help her wash some utensils. The pan that my mother uses to cook ugali normally creates a hard layer that I cannot remove easily. So, we put some water in the pan to soften it. We wash it in the evening. From 3:00 p.m., our mother allows us to watch movies up to 6:00 p.m.
