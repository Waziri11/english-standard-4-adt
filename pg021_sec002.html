Father and the shopkeeper

My name is Aika. One day, I went to the market with my father. At the market, many people were selling and buying things. My father bought some rice, fruits, and vegetables. We also went to one of the shops. The conversation between my father and the shopkeeper went as follows.

Father:

Good morning.

Shopkeeper:

Good morning. Can I help you?

Father:

Yes, please. I need some sugar.

Shopkeeper:

How much do you need?

Father:

Two kilograms, please. How much is it?

Shopkeeper:

Two thousand and five hundred shillings per kilo.

Is that all?

Father:

I also need one packet of salt, five litres of cooking oil, and a bar of soap.

Shopkeeper:

OK. Here they are.

Father:

Can I get my bill, please?

Shopkeeper:

Ten thousand shillings, please.

Father:

Here’s your money.

Shopkeeper:

Thank you and welcome again.

Father:

Goodbye.
