Kidoti comes late from school

Kidoti is a Standard Four pupil. She studies at Ipole Primary School. The school is three kilometres away from her home. She goes to school by a school bus. She usually wakes up at 5 a.m. to prepare herself for school. The school bus picks her up at around 6 a.m. The lessons begin at 7 a.m. and end at 4 p.m. However, on Wednesday last week, Kidoti came home very late and her mother was very worried. After she got home, the mother and the daughter had the following conversations.

Mother:Welcome back home, my daughter!
Daughter:Thank you very much, mum!
Mother:You are so late today and you look really tired!What happened?
Daughter:I am very sorry, mum.The teacher took us to a practical session and we finished it late.
