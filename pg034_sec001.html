Expressing past events

Introduction

Expressing past events helps us share stories, describe experiences, and explain what we did in the past. In this unit, you will learn to talk/sign about something that happened once, things that happened many times or over a longer period in the past. The competencies developed will enable you to express past events and tell clear and interesting stories through speech or sign language.

Think about the words you use to talk or sign about past events.
Activity 4.1: Expressing past events using simple past
Activity 4.1:
Expressing past events using simple past
(a)Study the following sentences and say/sign what you notice in the italicised words. The italicised words are: danced, watched, and walked.
1. I danced at the party.
2. Yesterday, I watched a football match.
3. Yesterday, I walked to school.
(b)Study the following sentences and say/sign what you notice in the italicised words. The italicised words are: went, ate, and wrote.
1. Mary went to Dodoma yesterday.
2. We ate pilau on Kemy’s birthday.
3. She wrote an email.
