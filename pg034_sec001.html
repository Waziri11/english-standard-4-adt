Expressing past events

Introduction

Expressing past events helps us share stories, describe experiences, and explain what we did in the past. In this unit, you will learn to talk about something that happened once, things that happened many times or over a longer period in the past. The competencies developed will enable you to express past events and tell clear and interesting stories about past experiences.
