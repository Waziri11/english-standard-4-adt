Activity 9.2:

Writing birthday wishes

(a) Study the following best wishes message.

Then, describe how the message looks like and what it is about.

Dear

I would like to send you warm regards as you celebrate your birthday. Happy birthday!

Mbuke

(b) Read the following birthday wishes card.

Dear Betty,

As you turn 9 today, I wish you every success in life.

Happy birthday, Betty!

Chakupewa

(c) Complete the following birthday wishes card.

Dear (name of your friend)
(wish him or her the best)
(sign your name)
