Class meeting in Mkulima Hall

Last month, Standard Four pupils at Mabo Primary School had a class meeting. The meeting took place in the Mkulima Hall near the school library. There were no enough chairs and tables inside the hall. Some pupils went outside the hall to get more chairs and tables. Hall attendants were busy supplying mineral water to the pupils. They put two bottles of water on each table. They also put small waste bins under each table. The meeting started a bit late at around 3 p.m. Some pupils had started jumping over the desks. The Standard Four class teacher had another meeting in the head teacher’s office. When she arrived, she greeted the pupils and apologised for coming late. She then declared the meeting open.

Questions

1. Where did the meeting take place?

2. Where did some pupils go to get more chairs and tables?

3. When did the meeting start?

4. Where did the hall attendants put the waste bins?

5. Where did the hall attendants put the bottles of water?

6. Why did the meeting start late?
