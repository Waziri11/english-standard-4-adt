English Pupil's Book Standard Four

(g) Practise with your fellow pupil how to use ‘some’ and ‘any’ in conversations. Let your fellow pupil ask you questions and you will respond using either ‘some’ or ‘any’. Remember.
1.
Use ‘some’ when offering or requesting things in a positive sentence. Example; “Can I have some water?”
2.
Use ‘any’ in negative sentences or when asking if something is available. Example; “Do we have any apples left?”
Now, start by letting your fellow ask you a question like “Do we have milk?” or “Would we like sugar in your tea?”
