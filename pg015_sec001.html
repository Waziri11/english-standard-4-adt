“Could you give me a discount?” She asked, smiling shyly.

Yobu chuckled. “For you, I can lower it to 35,000 shillings, but no less. The cost of getting these rice cookers from the factory has gone up!”

“Alright!” The woman agreed, handing him the money.

Back at Mwanaidi’s stall, Rejo began selling his grains, chatting with customers about the freshest produce in the market. As the day went on, the sellers and buyers continued their lively exchanges, each finding something they needed - be it a piece of cloth, a kitchen utensil, or a juicy guava.

By evening, the sellers began packing up, satisfied with another busy day at Tupendane Market.
