Taking part in conversations by talking/signing

Introduction
Taking part in spoken or signed conversations is important if you want to enhance your ability to express your ideas, build social relations and communicate. In this unit, you will learn to express ideas and ask questions by taking part in spoken or signed conversations. The competencies developed will enable you to become a more confident language user and active participant in different spoken or signed conversations.
Think about the way you can express your ideas and build social relations by taking part in spoken or signed conversations
Activity 6.1: Taking part in conversations at home by talking/signing
(a) Read the following text.
A hard-working daughter

My name is Ashura. Last Saturday, my mother went to work early in the morning. Before she left, she asked me to clean our compound, wash the dishes, and make up our beds. When she came back, it was already 2:00 p.m.; she started talking to me. Our conversation was as follows.

Mother: Hello, my daughter! How was your day?

Daughter: It was all fine. What about yours?

Mother: It was very good. Did you clean the compound?

Daughter: Yes, I tried but the broom was broken. I borrowed one from our neighbour, mama Hawa.

Mother: Great! What about the dishes? Did you wash them? Mother: Great! What about the dishes? Did you wash them?

Daughter: Yes, I did. I first went to fetch water from the tap. Daughter: Yes, I did.

Mother: Thanks a lot. But your bed isn’t in order. You didn’t make it, did you? Mother: Thanks a lot.

Daughter: No! I didn’t make it up because I washed all the covers. Daughter: No! I didn’t make it up because I washed all the covers.

Mother: Ok! You are a nice girl! Mother: Ok! You are a nice girl!

Daughter: Thank you, mum for the compliment. Daughter: Thank you, mum for the compliment.
