(b) Oddo is a Standard Four pupil at Kilimatinde Primary School. One day, he fell sick. He decided to go to Mwembeni District Hospital for medication. The following was the conversation between Oddo and the doctor.

Oddo falls sick

Doctor: Good morning! How can I help you?

Oddo: Good morning, doctor. My name is Oddo. I’ve been feeling a bit tired and dizzy lately.

Doctor: I see. Have you experienced any other symptoms, like headache or shortness of breath?

Oddo: Yes, I’ve had a few mild headaches, and I frequently experience breathing difficulties than usual.

Doctor: Okay, let’s check your blood pressure and do a few tests to get a better understanding. Have you been sleeping well?

Oddo: Not really. I’ve been waking up several times at night.

Doctor: That might be contributing to your dizziness and fatigue. We’ll run some tests and, depending on the results, administer your treatment. Go to the laboratory for blood and urine tests; then come back here.

Oddo: Thank you, doctor. I appreciate your advice.

Doctor: You’re welcome! I’ll get your test results soon and let you know. Take care.

Oddo: I will! Thanks.
