Act out with your fellow pupil the following dialogue. Then, describe the form of the verbs in bold.

Roza: Hi, Juma! How was your weekend?
Juma: It was great! I visited my grandparents in the village.
Roza: Did you buy them any gift?
Juma: Oh, yeah! I bought some gifts for them.
Roza: That sounds fun! What did you do there?
Juma:
I helped my grandpa with the garden, and we planted some flowers. We also went fishing at the lake.
Roza: Wow! Did you catch any fish?
Juma:
Yes, I caught two big ones! We cut and grilled them for dinner. We ate them with rice.
Roza: Well then! I stayed home, played, and watched a movie with my family.
Juma: Nice! What movie did you watch?
Roza:
We watched The King Lion. It was terrific.
Juma: How was it?
