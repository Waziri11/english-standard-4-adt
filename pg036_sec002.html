Activity 4.2: Asking and responding to questions in the past- simple past

(a) Study the following questions and their answers. Then, say/sign what you have observed in the answers.

1. Did John brush his teeth in the morning?

Yes, he brushed his teeth in the morning.

2. Did Neema comb her hair in the morning?

No, she did not comb her hair in the morning.

(b) Study the following other ways of answering questions.

1. Did Amina go to school last Saturday?

No, she did not.

2. Did Baraka cook ugali yesterday?

Yes, he did.

(c) Study the following ways of answering questions.

1. Did Asha read this story?

Yes.

2. Did Ali visit Mikumi National Park?

No.
