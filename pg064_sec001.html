Thieves break into Mashaka’s shop

Last week, thieves broke Mashaka’s shop. They took some properties including a weighing machine. Mashaka reported the incident to the nearby police station. The Police Officer interrogated Mashaka and their conversations were as follows:

Police officer:
Welcome, sir.
Mashaka:
Thank you, officer.
Police officer:
What can I do for you?
Mashaka:
I have come to report a burglary; thieves broke into my shop last night.
Police officer:
What time was it?
Mashaka:
A few minutes past midnight. Ten past twelve to be exact.
Police officer:
How many were they?
Mashaka:
I’m not sure. But, it must be more than one.
Police officer:
Did they take anything?
Mashaka:
Yes, of course. They took some properties including a weighing machine.
Police officer:
What is the estimate of the value of all the stolen items?
Mashaka:
Five or more million shillings.
Police officer:
And the weighing machine?
Mashaka:
About two million.
Police officer:
That’s a lot, I must say!
Mashaka:
Yes, indeed.
Police officer:
Do you suspect anybody?
Mashaka:
Yes! Two young men, Kipara and Kipepe are my suspects. They keep visiting my shop even when the shop is closed.
Police officer:
We will investigate and let you know.
