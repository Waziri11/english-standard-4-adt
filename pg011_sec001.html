A Visit to the Hospital

One day, Sarah felt sick. Her mother took her to a nearby hospital. When they arrived, a friendly nurse greeted them and guided them to see the doctor. In the doctor’s room they found the doctor in his laboratory coat seated on a chair. The doctor asked Sarah how she was feeling. He listened carefully to Sarah’s explanation. The doctor advised Sarah to go to the laboratory for some tests to find out what was wrong.

Sarah and her mother went to the laboratory. They found the laboratory technician also wearing a laboratory coat and gloves. He took a small sample of Sarah’s blood using a syringe. Sarah was nervous, but the laboratory technician was very kind and made her feel safe. After a few minutes, the technician gave them the test results. They took the test results to the doctor. The doctor explained that Sarah needed some medicine to help her get better. The doctor prescribed the medicine and gave them a piece of paper to take to the pharmacy.

They went to the hospital pharmacy. The pharmacist gave them the medicine. The pharmacist explained how Sarah should take the medicine. Sarah’s mother thanked the pharmacist and she promised him she will take care of the patient.
