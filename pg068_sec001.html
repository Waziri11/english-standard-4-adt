A phone call to Mrs Nyoni

Mrs Nyoni and her family live in the city centre. Every Saturday, she goes to the market. The market is in the city near a bus stand. Last Saturday, she took a bus to the market. She wanted to buy a lot of supplies for her home. Back home, there were no food, meat, and vegetables in the kitchen. She also needed to buy new plates, spoons, glasses and basins. When she got off the bus, she walked straight to the market. Suddenly, she got a phone call from her daughter, Maria. “Hello, mum, David just got an accident.” He fell and got hurt when he was playing football. “What” She shouted. “He got injured when playing football,” Maria replied. “Where is he now?” Mrs Nyoni asked. “He is in the sitting room getting first aid from our brother,” said Maria.
