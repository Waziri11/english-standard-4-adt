The bus arrived at Mkunguni in the evening. Allen and Pendo found their grandparents waiting for them. The grandparents were happy to see their grandchildren. They helped their grandchildren to carry their bags. When they got home, their grandmother started cooking dinner. She cooked chicken and rice. It was a very delicious meal. They all ate the food happily. After eating, they sat near the fire outside the house. Their grandfather told them many stories. One of the stories was about the hare and the hyena. Both the grandparents and the grandchildren had missed each other a lot. That night, all of them went to bed late.
