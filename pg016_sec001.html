Makelo at the bus station

Makelo travels regularly on business trips. He is well known by all bus drivers at Itilima bus station. In December, he wanted to travel to Pelalu village. He made an online booking two days before. He got seat number 3B. The ticket cost him Tsh. 20,000/=. The ticket indicated that reporting time was 5.30 a.m. and check in time 6.00 a.m. The departure time for the bus was written 6:30 a.m. and it was expected to travel for 6 hours. So, the arrival time was written 12:30 p.m. Although he had planned to travel on 24th December 2023, he postponed his trip because he fell sick before the travel date.

The next morning, he felt better and went to the bus station. He wanted to catch the morning bus. The bus ticket sales agent told him to travel in the afternoon because the morning bus was full. There were so many passengers that day. Makelo waited for the bus for over five hours. He eventually boarded the bus at 1:00 p.m. He was the first person to get into the bus. When he arrived in the village, he phoned his family and told them that he had arrived safely.
