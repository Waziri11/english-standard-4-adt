(b) Read the following text and answer the questions that follow.

Early morning routine

My name is Olewado. Every day, I wake up at 5:30 am. At 5:40 a.m., I cook my tea. At 6:00 a.m., I take my breakfast and wash the dishes thereafter. Since my parents wake up at 6:05 am, I thus have to greet them before I have a bath at 6:10 a.m. At 6:15 a.m., we all gather in the sitting room to say prayers. My brother and I put on school uniform immediately after that, ready for school at 6:25 a.m. We walk for about 30 minutes every morning.
