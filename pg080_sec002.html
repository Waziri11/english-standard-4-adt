Activity 9.3:
Writing congratulatory messages
(a) Read the following congratulatory message.

Dear Dad,

I know you have been promoted to a senior position
at work. Congratulations! I hope you will enjoy more
promotions in the future.

Doris

(b) Complete the following congratulatory message.
Dear (name of the person)
(congratulate him/her)
