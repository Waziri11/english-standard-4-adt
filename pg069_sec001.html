Mrs Nyoni stopped shopping.

Mrs Nyoni stopped shopping. She quickly went to the bus stop to catch a bus back home. There were so many travellers waiting for buses. There were a few buses coming to the stop. That was really a bad day for her. Mrs Nyoni became worried as she wanted to get home immediately to take David to hospital. She did not know what to do.

Luckily, Mrs Maganga, Mrs Nyoni’s neighbour, was nearby. She had parked her car near the bus stop. She called Mrs Nyoni, “Do you want to go home, Mrs Nyoni?” Mrs Nyoni looked back and saw Mrs Maganga calling. “Yes please, I want to go home but there are no buses to get me there”. “Don’t worry my neighbour, I can give you a ride,” Said Mrs Maganga. “Oh, thank you so much!” Mrs Nyoni said getting into Mrs Maganga’s car. They quickly drove to Mrs Nyoni’s home. They found David lying on the floor crying.

They took him into Mrs Maganga’s car. After a few minutes, they got to hospital. David was taken to the emergency section on a stretcher. He was examined by Dr Seko. Then, a nurse took him to the X-ray room for further examination. He had a fracture. The nurse wrapped around the fractured area with a Plaster of Paris (POP). David was given a bed rest. A few days later, David was discharged. He thanked his mother, Mrs Maganga, Dr Seko and the nurse for their help. After a few weeks, the POP was removed from the injured part.
