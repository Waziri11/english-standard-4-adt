Mrs Babu gets an accident

One day, Mrs Babu was crossing a road near a petrol station in the city centre. It was late in the evening. The lights had gone out. She stepped on a broken bottle and her leg got badly cut. She cried loudly calling for help and people came running to help her. They took her to a nearby hospital. In the doctor’s room, the dialogue went as follows.

Doctor: What’s your name?
Mrs Babu: Bahati Frank.
Doctor: What happened to your leg?
Mrs Babu: I stepped on a broken piece of a glass bottle.
Doctor: Oh sorry. I can see the cut. I am very sorry. I will call a nurse who will clean the cut and do some dressing for you.
Mrs Babu: Thank you, doctor.
Doctor: Is it very painful?
Mrs Babu: Yes, of course.
Doctor: Don’t worry, Mrs Babu. We will give you some pain killers.
Mrs Babu: Thank you, doctor. How much does it cost?
Doctor: Ten thousand shillings only. Pay at the cashier’s counter.
Mrs Babu: Okay. Thank you.
