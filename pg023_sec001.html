Ms Mambo and her pupils

Ms Mambo is a Standard Four English language teacher at Mtoni Primary School. One morning, she had a lesson. She was teaching about expressing quantity in English. All the pupils were very happy. Some of the pupils had already taken their pens. They wanted to start writing. Ms Mambo told them, “Don’t write anything now.” It will take some time before you start writing. She continued, “Some is used in positive sentences to indicate unspecified quantity. For example, I have some books. Any is used in negative sentences or questions to refer to an indefinite amount or number. For example, I don’t have any books or Do you have any books?”

Questions

  1. 1. Which words that express quantity did Ms Mambo use?
  2. 2. How was the word ‘some’ used?
  3. 3. How was the word ‘any’ used?
  4. 4. Make your own five sentences using ‘any’ and five sentences using ‘some’.
