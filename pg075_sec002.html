Activity 8.4:
Reading with appropriate stress and intonation

(a) Read aloud/sign the following text. Use the following instructions for reading/signing.

1. Stress the words meow, kitten, and home to emphasise their importance.

2. Use Amani’s gentle tone when speaking, or a gentle signing style, to show care and concern for the kitten.

3. Raise your tone slightly when asking questions to express curiosity.

4. End the story with a happy tone, which Amani used to show satisfaction in helping.
