Unit One: Developing vocabulary about everyday environment.
Introduction

We need many words to describe the things around us. In this unit, you will learn new words and use them to describe or name things found at home, school, markets, bus or railway stations, and in hospitals. The competencies developed will help you improve your English in speaking, signing and writing.

Think about the words you know and how you use them in communication.
