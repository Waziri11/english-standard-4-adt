Introduction

Sometimes we talk about something, which is the result of something else. We also talk about the reason for something to happen. In this unit, you will learn to express result and reason using ‘so’ and ‘because’. The competencies developed will enable you to express yourself better in English.

Think about the words that show a cause or a result of something
