Reading for comprehension

Introduction

When we read, we either want to obtain general or specific information. In this unit, you will learn to read to get a message from the text. The goal will be to get the skills of reading for general and specific information. The competencies developed will enable you to read and get the correct message from the text.

Think about the way you can read different texts to obtain general and specific information

Activity 7.1: Reading for general information

(a) Read/sign the following conversations between the teacher and the pupils in the classroom.

Teacher: Good morning, class!

Class: Good morning, Teacher!

Teacher: Today, we are going to learn about something very important when reading. Does anyone know what “reading for general information” means?

Pupil 1: Is it when you read a book?

Teacher: Good guess! Yes, it involves reading, but it’s a special type of reading. When we read for general information, we read to get a basic understanding of a topic. It’s like reading to know a little about something, not everything.
