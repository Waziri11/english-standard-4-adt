(c) Imagine your aunt and uncle are celebrating their 10th wedding anniversary.

They have always been kind and supportive of you.

Write or Braille a congratulatory message to them on this special occasion.

In your message, include:

1. A warm greeting.

2. A mention of their anniversary and how happy you are for them.

3. A personal touch, that means, something you admire about their relationship or a special memory you have with them.

4. A kind closing to end your message.
